What every signal carries

              
Instrument and direction ·
                entry · volume · stop · target · ticket · current status ·
                your disclaimer.

            
What it never carries

              
An invented exit price, a
                cause the broker did not report, a profit figure, or a win rate.
                If a number is not in the trade record, it is not printed.

            
Real signals are published by your signal bot.
              This studio previews the format; it does not post on its own.
